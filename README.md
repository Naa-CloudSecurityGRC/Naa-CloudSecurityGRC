### How I approach GRC
- **Risk first, checklist second.** Start from what could actually hurt the business, then pick the fewest controls that cover it.
- **Evidence over assertions.** A control is "done" only when there's evidence it operates.
- **Decisions, not just documents.** Every project records what I chose not to do and why, and who owns what's left.
